Vuigram

Multi-tenant online storefronts on Telegram

Vuigram lets merchants create and run online stores with a Telegram bot, Stripe and PayPal payments, PDF invoicing and a merchant dashboard.

Your online store where your customers already are: Telegram.

  • Status In development
  • Licence No open licence declared
  • Backend Go + Fiber
  • Data PostgreSQL 16, Redis and NATS
  • Deployment Docker Compose
Visit the site

What it is

A complete storefront inside Telegram

Vuigram is a multi-tenant SaaS platform: each merchant creates a store, publishes products and receives orders from a web storefront and a Telegram bot. The Go API handles authentication, catalogue, orders, payments and invoicing, while a worker processes background jobs through NATS JetStream.

Architecture

Storefront, API and asynchronous jobs

Next.js 15 (storefront and dashboard)
   |
   v
Go Fiber API :8080 ---> PostgreSQL 16
   |                   Redis 7
   |                   MinIO / S3
   v
NATS JetStream ---> worker (email, orders, stock, reports)
   |
   +---> Telegram bot

Docker Compose brings up PostgreSQL, Redis, NATS, MinIO, the API, the worker, the web app and Traefik as a reverse proxy.

Capabilities

From catalogue to fulfilled order

  • Multi-tenant

    Several stores per account

    Each store defines its type, country, language and currency, and its data stays separate from other merchants.

  • Telegram

    Built-in bot

    Menus, commands, webhook and order notifications inside the conversation itself.

  • Payments

    Stripe and PayPal

    Stripe PaymentIntent charges and PayPal orders with webhooks; cash on delivery is also supported.

  • Orders

    Checkout and statuses

    Multi-step guest checkout, order statuses, cancellation with stock replenishment and refunds.

  • Inventory

    Stock and variants

    Inventory movements, low-stock alerts, product variants and CSV import and export.

  • Invoicing

    PDF and email

    PDF invoices with per-store configuration, preview and email delivery.

  • Growth

    Coupons and affiliates

    Coupon CRUD, checkout validation and tracking of sales attributed to affiliates.

  • Dashboard

    Merchant control

    Analytics, customers, products, orders, inventory, invoices and settings from a single dashboard.

  • Storefront

    Editor and effects

    Per-store templates and animated effects, with legal pages and public order tracking.
