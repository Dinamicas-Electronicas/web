01 — Infrastructure

Expanse

The visual IDE for Docker Compose

Expanse is a self-hosted web application for designing, deploying and operating Docker Compose stacks without writing YAML by hand. Drag services onto the canvas, connect them, adjust ports, variables and resources, and the app validates conflicts, generates the Compose file and runs it against the host's Docker Engine.

The library brings together 142 templates across 14 categories, 25 complete stacks and high-availability variants with HAProxy, replicas and PgBouncer. The same panel shows metrics and logs, opens a terminal per container and shares read-only links with an expiry date.

  • Visual canvasReact Flow and ELK.js to order the architecture.
  • Real DockerValidates ports and runs docker compose up on the host.
  • ObservabilityLive status, CPU, memory and logs.
  • SecurityJWT, TOTP MFA, revocable sessions and auditing.

02 — Commerce and communication

Vuigram

Multi-tenant online stores on Telegram

Vuigram is a multi-tenant SaaS platform for building online stores inside Telegram. Each merchant publishes a catalogue, receives orders from a web storefront and a bot, and manages customers, stock, invoices and settings from a single panel.

The Go API handles authentication, catalogue, orders, payments and invoicing; a worker processes background jobs with NATS JetStream. Stripe and PayPal cover payments, while the system generates PDF invoices, tracks variants and inventory movements, applies coupons and attributes sales to affiliates.

  • Multi-tenantMultiple stores per account, with separated data.
  • PaymentsStripe PaymentIntent and PayPal orders with webhooks.
  • InventoryStock, variants, alerts and CSV import.
  • InvoicingPDF per store, preview and email delivery.

03 — Data and language

RSS2

Multilingual news archive to leave the echo chamber behind

RSS2 gathers thousands of RSS and Atom feeds published by outlets around the world, keeps the original text and translates it into Spanish on your own machine with NLLB-200. It then extracts entities, computes embeddings and groups the news that tell the same story so you can compare them.

The idea is simple: if you only read what an algorithm picks for you, you end up living in an echo chamber. RSS2 goes to the source, shows who says what and leaves interpretation in your hands. Search combines semantic similarity with date, topic, country and category filters, and the optional voice bulletin turns a selection of stories into a newsreel with an editable script and two approvals.

It installs with Docker or as native Windows 11 services, with portable PostgreSQL and no Redis or nginx. The models work offline and your data never leaves your machine.

  • IngestionGo, every 15 minutes and without duplicates.
  • Local translationNLLB-200 on CPU or GPU, no cloud.
  • Semantic searchpgvector, entities and combined filters.
  • Docker or Windows 11Containers or native services.

04 — Communication and spaces

ChatLand

XMPP chat with persistent 3D zones

ChatLand is an Android XMPP messaging client in which every MUC room keeps its own 3D space. Identity, messages and the shared place live in the same room: positions, seats and furniture are decided on the server and represented in the app.

Prosody handles identity, membership and messages; Colyseus decides position, seats, objects and layout; Android provides the account, media, calls and notifications; Godot only presents the state and sends intentions. The app and the 3D process communicate through Binder via SpatialBridgeService.

  • Persistent MUC roomsIdentity and messages over XMPP.
  • Authoritative 3D roomGodot 4.7.2 and Colyseus in a separate process.
  • MediaAttachments, whiteboards, 1:1 voice and zone voice.
  • GamesSix turn-based games arbitrated in Lua.
