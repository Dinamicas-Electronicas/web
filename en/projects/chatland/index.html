ChatLand

XMPP chat with persistent 3D zones

An Android XMPP messaging client where every MUC room keeps its own 3D space: the same identity, the same messages and a shared place with positions, seats and furniture.

The conversation and the space are the same room: you join to talk and share a place.

What it is

XMPP messaging and an authoritative 3D world

ChatLand combines an XMPP client with a spatial layer on Colyseus and a room rendered by Godot 4.7.2. Prosody handles identity, room membership and messages; Colyseus decides position, seats and interaction; Android provides account, media and calls; Godot only presents state and sends intents. There is no second 3D engine and no game state in the client.

  • Identity

    XMPP accounts with Smack

    Sign-up and login, persistent profile and avatar, and local lock with PIN, password or biometrics.

  • Chat

    Persistent MUC rooms

    Direct messaging and MUC rooms, channel discovery, invitations and an XEP-0055 directory.

  • 3D room

    Godot 4.7.2 in its own process

    Movement, collisions, seats, screens, doors and frames in a process separate from the rest of the app.

  • Layout

    Authoritative furniture

    The server decides the layout; the client edits it with optimistic revision and change history.

  • Media

    Attachments and whiteboards

    Images, GIF, video, audio, voice notes, PDF and documents, with several attachments per message and signed HTTPS uploads.

  • Voice

    Calls and zone voice

    1:1 calls over WebRTC and mesh zone voice, with optional TURN for strict NAT.

  • Cineforum

    Synchronised playback

    Shared screens and coordinated playback inside the zone.

  • Games

    Six turn-based games

    Tic-tac-toe, connect four, battleship, snakes and ladders, hangman and parchís, refereed in Lua inside Prosody.

  • Bridge

    App and Godot over Binder

    SpatialBridgeService validates and relays what the Godot HUD sends: chat, attachments, voice and layout.

Architecture

Four authorities that do not overlap

Prosody    identity, MUC membership, messages and tokens
Colyseus   position, seats, objects, layout and validation
Android    account, media, calls and notifications
Godot      presentation, HUD and audio (no authoritative state)

The app and the :godot process communicate over Binder through SpatialBridgeService. Spatial protocol v1 is the safe default and v2 is enabled per zone.

Status

A functional lab, not ready for the Internet

Version 0.53.0 (versionCode 59) was verified on 19 November 2026 against the repository tree:

  • 320/320 Android unit tests and 118/118 spatial server tests with test Redis and PostgreSQL.
  • 13 scenes and 32 avatars with validated budgets; navigation 13/13 in the asset gate.
  • Six lab containers in healthy state: Prosody, Colyseus, Redis, PostgreSQL, uploads and proxy.

Known limits

  • No end-to-end encryption, external audit, backups or multi-node deployment.
  • No MAM archive, push notifications or group calls with an SFU.
  • Google sign-in derives the XMPP password on the client; Prosody does not validate the token.
  • Incomplete MUC moderation from the UI and no instrumented tests in CI.

Declared debt

  • End-to-end encryption and security audit
  • MAM archive and push notifications
  • Full MUC moderation: kick, ban and affiliation
  • Group calls with an SFU and multi-node deployment with a load balancer
  • Signed release and instrumented tests in CI
